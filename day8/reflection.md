# Day 8 – Course Reflection

## 1. What was the most difficult concept in the course, and how did you overcome it?

The most difficult concept for me was system design and understanding how applications handle large numbers of users at the same time. Concepts such as load balancing, caching, database relationships and scalability were initially challenging. I overcame this by practising with the daily assignments, researching unfamiliar concepts and breaking complex problems into smaller parts. Working on the QuickNotes and SnapShare projects also helped me understand how these concepts apply in real-world applications.

## 2. Which part of your capstone would you improve, based on the feedback you received?

I would improve the database design and architecture documentation of my QuickNotes capstone. I would provide more detailed explanations of how database queries are handled, how indexes improve performance and how the system responds when traffic increases. I would also improve the architecture diagram to make the communication between components clearer.

## 3. What will you learn next?

I plan to focus on Data Analysis and develop my skills in collecting, cleaning, analysing and interpreting data. I want to learn more about Excel, SQL, Power BI and Python for data analysis. I am also interested in data visualization and presenting insights that can support better decision-making. My goal is to become a skilled data analyst who can use data to solve real-world problems and make informed decisions.
